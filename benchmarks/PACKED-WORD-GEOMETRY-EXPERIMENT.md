## Execution lead

One two-second CPU0/CPU1 capture has1,996 sample locations: main packed execution
1,771, geometry builder132, kernel68 and geometry reader7. The horizontal tap
loop repeatedly loads packed UInt32 products, widens them to double and prepares
each pair's input views. JIT confirms that actual structure. Locations, inlining
and sampling skid are not phase times or removable percentages. All instrumented
elapsed is discarded; the capture is not a timing confirmation.

The next architectural design will extend the existing byte decoded-cache prefix
to word pixels and consolidate the two packed production classes. Exact word
products fit UInt32 and convert exactly to double; preparing them once per source
pixel could avoid repeated conversion during horizontal accumulation. Reuse the
existing result-tail cache, retain the packed suffix and each depth's numerical
certificates/repair, and retire prefix users before suffix publication. This is
a hypothesis requiring complete-operation evidence, not automatic acceptance.
