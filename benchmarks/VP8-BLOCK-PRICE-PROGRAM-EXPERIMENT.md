# VP8 block price program experiment

The full-block price program establishes no useful complete-operation gain and is
restored to the qualified implementation. Exact packets and focused correctness
pass. This rejects one implementation without closing revised preparation designs.

The prototype prepares the current block's candidate transition and terminal prices
before its serial two-state recurrence. A 72-byte step holds rates, distortion
and terminal costs plus floor/choice count. Sixteen steps remain local to one call;
rate epochs, ownership, zero exits, candidate order, arithmetic, strict ties and
traceback are unchanged. No heap allocation, cache, pool or escaping state is added.
