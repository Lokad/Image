# WebP matching preparation

Three coherent stages separate admitted forward preparation from a borrowed
reverse worker, cache the current pruning sample, prepare paired source windows,
then reject candidates that cannot beat the best length without locating their
earlier mismatch. The last stage uses bounded Boolean prefix equality and exact
viable tails. No buffer, owner, cache or numerical policy is added.

These quiet-VM screens establish **no useful gain**. Wide intervals, small leads
and adverse normalized points are retained. Later stages reuse the initial before
to decide continuation; they are not independent confirmations. Restore production
without further sampling. Qualified costs remain unchanged; no causal regression,
CPU-parity or improvement claim follows from these points.

Complete packets stay exact at 98,590 and 160,032 B. Allocation remains 8,622,560
and 6,516,744 B/op in these short jobs. This is not a heap-peak or retention
measurement. Candidate order, distances, ties, alias-safe reverse writes,
cancellation and admission remain checked. The last stage coalesces redundant
inner cancellation polls into bounded 256-sample Boolean comparisons, without
letting a partial index escape; that implementation is also restored.

One current two-second instruction capture motivated the redesign: 286 of 1,961
process samples map to matcher Build, including 260 in reverse search, 212 in
the candidate/pruning/inlined-prefix region and 64 in its scalar prefix loop.
Method-only prefix counts miss that inline copy. Regions and sample skid are
attribution, not component times or predicted savings. Native source inspection
informs the query strategy only; no upstream source or table is copied.

Each stage passes 800 internal / 194 public WebP checks per normal/scalar mode
on Windows/Linux. Initial stage-one private-delegate binding failures are fixed
in tests without a core rebuild; failed logs remain. The original implementation
and actual qualified DLL/package are restored on both systems. Retain one new
actual-production regression: a closer three-sample match must not hide an older
five-sample match after the pruning sample changes. The restored implementation
passes 800 internal checks per mode and OS. Its unchanged production reuses
